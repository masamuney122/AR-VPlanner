Izmir has a subtropical Mediterranean climate. Summers are hot and rainless yet humid, so July and August highs of 30-35°C feel more like 40°C. Winters are mild to cool, around 12-16°C with occasional downpours. Spring and fall are warm and pleasant and generally the best time to visit. Beach holidays are best in fall, as the sea temperature lags a month or so behind the land, and is a balmy 20°C in September and October.

Konak Square is the city's main square and gives its name to the central district. The clocktower (Saat Kulesi), erected in 1901, is one of the most iconic landmarks of Izmir and has been repeatedly restored. Konak Mosque is a small octagonal mosque within the square, built in 1755 and decorated with Kütahya tiles.

Izmir Archaeological Museum, Halil Rifat Paşa Cd 4, Bahribaba Parkı. Daily 08:30-17:30. Displays a large collection of finds from Smyrna and other Ionian cities, mostly Roman. There's also a numismatic section with ancient coins and jewellery. Adult 90 TL.

Asansör (Elevator), Şehit Nihatbey Cd. Daily 06:00-00:00. This historic lift was constructed in 1907 to carry people between Karataş waterfront and the properties on the hill. It has two cars each taking about ten people on a 58 m lift. The restaurant at the top has a bird-eye view of Izmir. Free.

Hisar Mosque was built in the 1590s and is a striking example of Islamic architecture. Nearby mosques include Şadırvanaltı (1636), Başdurak (1652) within Kemeraltı Bazaar, and Kemeraltı Mosque (1671).

Smyrna Agora, Tarık Sarı Sk 29. Daily 08:30-17:30. Ruins of the marketplace of Hellenistic and Roman Smyrna. Adult 200 TL.

Atatürk Museum, Atatürk Cd 248. Daily 08:30-17:00. Neo-classical mansion with rooms used by Atatürk. Free.

History and Art Museum, Kültür Park Fuar Alanı. Mostly Hellenistic statues, ceramics and coins. Adult 400 TL.

Kadifekale. Open 24 hours. Around 330 BC during the reign of Alexander the Great, the population fortified this hilltop. The present walls are medieval, though you can still see the Roman cisterns that supplied the city's water. Free.

Teleferik, Sakarya Cd 127. Built in 1977, this gondola lifts you 423 m up the hill. There are restaurants, cafes and gift shops at the top. Return ride 20 TL.

Izmir Bird Paradise (Kuş Cenneti), Tuzla, 32 km northwest of centre. Open 24 hours. Birdwatching park in the salt marshes of Gediz Delta, one of Turkey's largest coastal wetlands. Over 250 bird species, most popular are the flamingos. You have to leave your car at the parking lot, bring a bike to explore further.

Key Museum, Özgörkey Cd, Torbalı. W-Su 10:00-17:00. Large collection of antique cars and motorbikes. Adult 200 TL.

Kordon is what everyone calls the waterfront promenade of Atatürk Cd. It's lined by tall apartments and palm trees on one side and the Aegean on the other, with a broad strip of lawn and a cobbled street between.

Beaches: not downtown, which has a pleasant waterfront but not a beach. You need to get 20+ km west to the suburb of Güzelbahçe, and the coast improves further west towards Çeşme. North of the city is muddy lagoons, with the nearest resort at Foça.

Kemeraltı is the atmospheric main bazaar, in the streets east of Konak Square. Open M-Sa 08:00-19:00. Kızlarağası Han next to Hisar Mosque was a caravanserai built in 1745 and now housing shops.

Local food specialties: Kumru is a warm sandwich made with special sesame bread, Turkish sausage, grilled cheese and tomatoes. Boyoz is a local oily pastry, great with tea for breakfast. Tulum Peyniri is a cheese made in the Izmir region. Fish, typically grilled sea bass, is popular.

Sultan Yemek Evi, 1441st Sk 9/B. M-Sa 08:00-18:00. Reliable budget eatery.

Piraye Ev Yemekleri, 851st Sk 6B. M-Sa 07:00-17:00. Friendly inexpensive cafe with home-style cooking.

Köfteci Murat, 927th Sk 4. M-Sa 11:00-20:00. Serving good filling meatballs.

Komposto, 861st Sk 46. M-F 11:30-16:00. Bright modern place for Turkish and Aegean cuisine, with vegetarian options.

Manisalı Birtat, Kıbrıs Şehitleri Cd 82. M-Sa 11:30-23:00. The specialty is Manisa kebab, skewered meat drenched in yoghurt and tomato sauce.

Topçu, Vali Kazım Dirik Cd 3/B. Daily 08:00-01:30. Reliable restaurant for Turkish and Mediterranean food.

Tavacı Recep Usta Alsancak, Atatürk Cd 364. Daily 11:30-23:30. Pricey but quality food.

Soirée, Atatürk Cd 19. Daily 09:00-01:00. Pleasant fish restaurant on Konak pier.

Alsancak district is a grid of small streets with lots of cafes and bars in old Greek houses. 1448th Sk has end-to-end bars and pubs with outdoor seating. Nightlife is found on Kıbrıs Şehitleri Cd and Gazi Kadinlar Cd, especially at weekends.

Hotel Baylan, 1299th Sk 8, 100 m SW of Basmane station. Basic central place. B&B double 1500 TL.

Mithras Hotel, 1472nd Sk 4. Clean and comfy. B&B double 2000 TL.

Piano Hotel Izmir, 1364th Sk 11. Clean and central, tiny rooms. B&B double 3000 TL.

Ibis Izmir Alsancak, Atatürk Cd 410, facing Alsancak station. Pleasant budget chain, rooms are small but clean. B&B double 2500 TL.

Doubletree by Hilton, 1373rd Sk 5. Central. B&B double 4000 TL.

Renaissance Izmir Hotel, Gazi Osman Paşa Blv 16. Comfy and friendly, 500 m from the waterfront. B&B double 6000 TL.

Ege Palas, Cumhuriyet Blv 210. Clean and efficient, great views from upper rooms. B&B double 5000 TL.

Key Hotel, Atatürk Cd 36. Slick waterfront hotel with restaurant and gym. B&B double 6000 TL.

Swissotel Buyuk Efes Izmir, Gazi Osman Paşa Blv 1. Great comfort, service and location at this waterfront hotel. B&B double 4000 TL.

Nearby destinations: Çeşme is a small harbour half an hour's drive west, with ferries to Chios. Selçuk to the south has many historical sights and is the hub for reaching the must-see Roman city of Ephesus. Şirince is a pleasant inland village renowned for its wines. Bergama to the north has the ruins of Pergamon, among the top sights in the country.
